# BalkanGuess

BalkanGuess is a daily music-guessing game for fans of Balkan music. Each day
offers a new song in every category and challenges players to identify it from
an increasingly longer audio clip.

## How it works

Players begin with a short snippet and have up to six guesses to name the song.
Each unsuccessful attempt unlocks more of the clip, turning every round into a
race between recognition and deduction. A player can skip an attempt when they
do not know the answer.

## Daily categories

The game includes a main Balkan Club Mix challenge plus focused Jala i Buba and
EXYU categories. Every category has its own daily song and saved progress, so
players can enjoy each challenge independently.

## Results and privacy

BalkanGuess saves a player's in-progress round in their browser. It also
collects anonymous aggregate results to show how the community performed, with
no account required to play.

## Music rights

Audio used by the project must be sourced, processed, and hosted with the
required permission from the relevant rights holders.
